Rex privacy policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026.

Rex is a personal assistant used only by its owner, Manny. It has no other users.

  • What it reads: the owner's own Google Nest devices (thermostat settings, and camera and doorbell events) through Google's Smart Device Management API, after the owner allows it on Google's consent screen.
  • Where it goes: onto the owner's own computer. It is never sold, shared, or used for ads.
  • Google user data is used only to show and control the owner's own devices, as Google's API Services User Data Policy requires.
  • How to stop it: remove Rex's access at myaccount.google.com, under Security, Third-party apps.
